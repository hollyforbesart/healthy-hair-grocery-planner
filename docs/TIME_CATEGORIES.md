# Time categories

Each meal has a `timeCategory` in `src/data/library/meals.json`:

- `quick` — about 15 minutes or less
- `moderate` — about 16–30 minutes
- `more-involved` — generally over 30 minutes

## How they were estimated

- Realistic home preparation, including cooking. Raw chicken, beef, fish, rice, quinoa, pasta and dry lentils are cooked from scratch; nothing is assumed to be precooked.
- Canned beans and chickpeas are treated as canned (the usual grocery form, and how the roasted-chickpea note uses them). Frozen edamame includes a few minutes to cook.
- Oven meals include preheating. Things that can cook at the same time (rice while the chicken cooks) overlap.
- Make-ahead dishes (overnight oats, chia pudding) are rated by hands-on time and flagged below.

These are estimates for planning, not tested recipe times. **Please review the flagged meals**; changing one is a one-word edit in `meals.json`.
