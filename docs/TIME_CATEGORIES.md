# Time categories

Each meal has a `timeCategory` in `src/data/library/meals.json`:

- `quick` — about 15 minutes or less (green clock)
- `moderate` — about 16–30 minutes (amber clock)
- `more-involved` — generally over 30 minutes (berry clock)

## How they were estimated

- Realistic home preparation, including cooking. Raw chicken, beef, fish, rice, quinoa, pasta and dry lentils are cooked from scratch; nothing is assumed to be precooked.
- Canned beans and chickpeas are treated as canned (the usual grocery form, and how the roasted-chickpea note uses them). Frozen edamame includes a few minutes to cook.
- Oven meals include preheating. Things that can cook at the same time (rice while the chicken cooks) overlap.
- When a meal sits on the line between two categories, it goes in the longer one, so nobody is steered wrong.

These are estimates for planning, not tested recipe times. **Waiting on Holly's suggestions to reconcile.**

## Make ahead

Meals with `"makeAhead": true` take little hands-on time but need a few hours or overnight to soak or set. They're rated by hands-on time and the card also shows **☾ Make ahead**.

- Strawberry Overnight Oats (Quick)
- Peanut Butter Banana Overnight Oats (Quick)
- Chia Pudding with Mango + Pumpkin Seeds (Quick)
- Chocolate Raspberry Chia Pudding (Quick)

## Quicker if eggs are boiled ahead

These include hard-boiling the eggs (~20 min), so they're Moderate. Boiling eggs ahead is a shortcut, not a requirement, so it belongs in the meal's preparation cue rather than a label.

- Boiled Eggs + Orange Slices (Moderate)
- Chickpea + Egg Salad (Moderate)
- Egg + Avocado Salad Sandwich (Moderate)

## Decisions on borderline meals

| Meal | Category | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Lentil + Spinach Soup with Whole-Grain Toast | Moderate → More involved | Cautious: dry lentils can take 35+ min. A prep cue can say it is quicker with canned or red lentils. |
| Mac + Cheese with Chicken + Broccoli | Moderate → More involved | Cautious: right at 30 min stovetop, longer if baked. |
| Jerk Chicken Pasta | Moderate → More involved | Cautious: 25–30 min, right on the line. |
| Protein Waffles with Berries + Greek Yogurt | Quick → Moderate | Waffle iron heat-up plus batches. |
| Turkey Chili Rice Bowl | More involved (kept) | Simmer time varies; kept on the cautious side. |
| Turkey + Kidney Bean Chili | More involved (kept) | Simmer time varies; kept on the cautious side. |
| Lentil Bolognese with Whole-Wheat Pasta | More involved (kept) | Assumes dry lentils. A prep cue can mention canned lentils. |
| Sweet Potato + Black Bean Bowl | More involved (kept) | Assumes roasted sweet potato. |
| Lentil + Roasted Vegetable Grain Bowl | More involved (kept) | Roasting makes it over 30 min. |
