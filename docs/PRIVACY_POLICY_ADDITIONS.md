## 2. Add to "1. Information We Collect"

**Purchase Information**

> When you purchase a digital product, such as Mane Meals: Meal & Grocery Planner, we collect your name, email address, the product purchased, the purchase date and amount, and related order details. Payments are processed by our e-commerce provider and its payment processor. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

**Mane Meals Account Information**

> To use the Mane Meals planner, you sign in with your email address. We store your email address, how and when your account was created, and the days on which you used the planner, so we can provide access, help you if you have trouble signing in, and understand how many people use the planner and whether they return. To sign in, we email you a one-time code. We do not use this information for advertising.

**Mane Meals Planner Information**

> The Mane Meals planner saves your plan, including foods you have on hand, staples, favorite meals, foods you choose to leave out, selected meals, grocery lists, your meal schedule (including anything you type into it), and meal history, to your Mane Meals account, so it is available on any device where you sign in. This information is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. A copy is also kept in your web browser's local storage on each device you use, so the planner works without an internet connection; signing out removes it from that device.
>
> We use your saved plan only to provide the planner to you. We do not use it for advertising and do not look at individual plans except when you ask us for help with your account.
>
> If you choose to submit feedback through the planner, we collect your rating and the answers you type. Feedback is sent to us by email. Please do not include sensitive medical information in feedback.

## 4. Add to "5. Third-Party Service Providers"

> **Kit**
> Kit may be used to sell and deliver digital products, process purchases and payments through its payment processor, send purchase confirmations and product-related emails, and manage subscriber information. Information processed through Kit may include your name, email address, purchase details, and subscriber status.
>
> **Cloudflare**
> Cloudflare hosts the Mane Meals planner and may process technical information, such as IP address, browser and device information, and request details, as necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the planner. Cloudflare also stores Mane Meals account information, saved plans, and the planner's anonymous usage statistics, described under Cookies and Analytics.
>
> **Resend**
> Resend sends the one-time sign-in codes for the Mane Meals planner. Information processed includes your email address and the content of the sign-in email.
>
> **Web3Forms**
> Web3Forms may be used to deliver feedback submitted through the Mane Meals planner to our email inbox. Information processed may include your rating, the answers you provide, and technical information needed to send the message.

## 5. Add to "6. Cookies and Analytics"

> The Mane Meals planner does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only to save your plan on your device, as described above.
>
> When you sign in, the planner sets one strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in on that browser for up to one year. It contains an account number and a security signature, not your name or email address, and is not used for advertising. If you clear it, you'll be asked to sign in again.

**Planner usage statistics** (active since October 17, 2026):

> To understand how the Mane Meals planner is used and improve it, the planner records anonymous usage events, such as pages and steps opened, meals and foods added, filters and searches used, and whether a grocery list was shared, copied, or printed. Each event is stored with Cloudflare along with the date and time and a general device type (phone, computer, or installed app). These events do not include your name, email address, IP address, a cookie or other identifier, your saved plan, or anything you type into your grocery list or feedback. Search terms you type in the meal search may be recorded in shortened form, so please don't type personal information there. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, the planner does not record usage events.

If you turn on Cloudflare Web Analytics later, also add:

> We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the planner is used, such as the number of visits and pages viewed. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and reports information in aggregate.

## 6. Add to "8. Data Retention"

> Purchase records are kept as long as needed to provide access to your purchase, provide support, and maintain business, tax, and accounting records. Plans saved in the Mane Meals planner are kept with your account until you use the planner's "Start over" option or ask us to delete your account; the copy in your browser is removed when you sign out or clear your browser data. Account information is kept while you have access to Mane Meals; you can ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting us. Anonymous usage statistics are kept to understand trends over time; because they are not linked to any person, they cannot be looked up or deleted for an individual.
